The house

A proper pub on South Quay

We are a traditional, family-run pub on Strand Street, South Quay in Padstow. Come in for lunch, an evening supper, or a night of live acoustic music.

Food is freshly made and locally sourced. At the bar we pour Cornwall’s speciality spirits, small batch brews, wines and cocktails.

Families and dogs are always welcome. We are open seven days a week.
